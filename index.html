07

Trail questions.

The things people usually ask about this Grit Overland GO A/T.

Is the Grit Overland GO A/T four-wheel drive?

It runs on Ford's full-time intelligent all-wheel-drive Transit platform with the EcoBoost twin-turbo V6. Grit then adds a 2″ lift, Falcon adjustable-reservoir shocks, skid plates and all-terrain tires. It isn't a low-range rock crawler, but it handles snowy passes, washboard gravel and forest service roads with ease.

Can you camp in it in winter?

Yes. It has four-season insulation, a fuel-fired heater that runs off the van's own fuel tank, and a heated grey tank, so the plumbing won't freeze overnight. The snow photos in the logbook are from real winter trips.

How long can it stay off-grid?

The 10,240 Wh lithium battery, 3,000 W inverter and dual-alternator charging will run the fridge, lights, laptop and induction cooktop for days. Driving tops the battery back up. Try the off-grid calculator above to estimate it for the way you camp.

Does it have air conditioning?

Yes, a 12V air conditioner that runs off the battery, so there's no generator or hookup needed. That's what makes desert summers and parking-lot afternoons bearable.

Can you work remotely from it?

Definitely. There's a stowaway bench seat, a dedicated work surface and a bamboo dining table, plus plenty of power for laptops, monitors and a hotspot.

Where is the van and how do I see it?

It's at Poulsbo RV, 12218 Highway 99 South, Everett, WA 98204, being sold on consignment. Contact the dealer and mention stock #CCX5588 to set up a walkthrough or test drive.

What does consignment mean for me as a buyer?

The van belongs to a private owner, but the dealer handles the sale. You get dealer-run test drives, paperwork and title transfer, and their financing options (subject to credit approval) instead of a cash-in-a-parking-lot private sale.
